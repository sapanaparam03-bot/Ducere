# Milestone 10 — API & Provider Licensing

Status: Inventory complete; final commercial licensing confirmation remains external.

## Known boundary
Ducere does not own third-party API data, provider names/logos, or third-party artwork merely because the app displays them.

## Watchmode
The current integration is server-side through the Supabase Edge Function. Commercial use must use a Watchmode plan whose terms permit the intended commercial use.

## TMDB
If TMDB data/services are used in a commercial product, the applicable commercial licensing terms must be confirmed. Non-commercial attribution requirements do not by themselves establish commercial rights.

## Cannot be completed remotely
Only the service provider and the eventual legal/commercial agreement can confirm the exact commercial rights, plan, attribution, redistribution, caching, and branding permissions for the final product.

## Release gate
Do not represent third-party provider/API rights as fully cleared until the applicable commercial terms are confirmed.

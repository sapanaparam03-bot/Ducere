# Ducere Brand Guidelines

## Brand status
Ducere's current visual identity is an original project asset created for the product. This document records the intended use of the identity; it is not trademark clearance.

## Core identity
- Name: Ducere
- Primary display: lowercase `ducere`
- Tone: cinematic, calm, personal, editorial
- Product idea: one place to track what you watched, what you want to watch, what you are watching, and where you can watch it.

## Core colors
- Background: #0d0f18
- Surface: #151722
- Primary accent: #e47a58
- Warm secondary: #d5af71
- Main text: #f3e9d5
- Muted text: #777989

## Assets
- `public/ducere-mark.svg` — app/brand mark
- `public/ducere-wordmark.svg` — horizontal wordmark
- `public/favicon.svg` — browser/favicon mark

## Usage
Use the mark on dark or neutral surfaces with sufficient contrast. Prefer the wordmark for larger brand placements and the mark for compact navigation/app-icon contexts.

Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, add effects to, or redraw the logo.

## Third-party assets
Streaming-provider logos, movie/TV/anime artwork, actor images, and other catalog imagery are not Ducere-owned merely because the app displays them. Their licensing/provenance remains tracked separately.

## Clearance
Before public commercial launch or a transaction, perform final trademark/name clearance in the intended jurisdictions and confirm rights for all third-party artwork and provider branding.

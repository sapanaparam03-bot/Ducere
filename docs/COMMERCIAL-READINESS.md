# Ducere — Commercial Readiness

Last reviewed: 2026-09-20

## Objective

Prepare Ducere for a real commercial conversation without spending heavily before there is genuine interest.

## Current position

### Product
- Working production architecture exists across GitHub, Vercel and Supabase.
- Core tracking, discovery, progress, availability, profile, settings, archive/import/export and account deletion functionality is implemented.
- Final authenticated browser/mobile QA remains a release step.

### Ownership
- Original Ducere source/product materials are documented in `docs/IP-OWNERSHIP-REGISTER.md`.
- Third-party software and service rights are documented separately.

### Third-party rights
- Dependency inventory exists.
- Asset provenance audit exists.
- Watchmode/TMDB/commercial licensing questions remain open until the exact production provider/plan and asset sources are finalized.

### Privacy
- Privacy Policy, Terms and About/Credits pages are implemented.
- Account export/import and deletion flows exist.
- A final legal/privacy review is deferred until commercial interest or public scale warrants it.

### Security
- Security audit exists.
- RLS and Edge Function JWT protection are verified.
- One Supabase platform warning remains: leaked-password protection is unavailable on the current setup.
- Dependency lockfile and full external security testing remain final-release items.

## Commercial blockers to resolve before a transaction

1. Choose and clear the final product name.
2. Confirm commercial rights for every production API/data source.
3. Confirm image/poster/backdrop/provider-logo usage rights.
4. Freeze dependencies and archive the exact release build.
5. Perform final browser/mobile QA.
6. Establish a legal entity and execute any needed IP assignments if other contributors exist.
7. Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal advice before signing a sale/partnership agreement.

## Pitch readiness

A lightweight first-contact package should contain:
- live demo URL;
- 30–60 second product demo;
- one-page product overview;
- problem/solution explanation;
- feature summary;
- architecture summary;
- founder/contact information;
- clear statement of what conversation is being requested.

Do not disclose secrets, private user data, service credentials, or unnecessary proprietary implementation details in the initial outreach.

## Diligence package to prepare only after genuine interest

- source repository snapshot;
- deployment/configuration inventory;
- architecture diagram;
- database schema;
- dependency/license inventory;
- asset provenance register;
- IP ownership register;
- privacy/terms;
- security audit;
- usage/traction metrics;
- financial model;
- vendor/API agreements;
- domain/trademark records;
- incorporation and assignment documents.

## Commercial-readiness conclusion

Ducere is being prepared for a credible initial commercial conversation. It is not being represented as legally transaction-ready until third-party rights, final QA, and formal legal diligence are completed.

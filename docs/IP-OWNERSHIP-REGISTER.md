# Ducere — Intellectual Property & Ownership Register

Last reviewed: 2026-09-20

## Purpose

This register records the intellectual-property assets that must be controlled and documented before Ducere is offered for investment, sale, acquisition, licensing, or commercial operation.

This document is an internal diligence record. It is not a legal opinion and does not itself transfer ownership.

## 3. Development history / AI-assisted development

Ducere has been developed with assistance from AI/software-building tools, including ChatGPT, Lovable and Replit during earlier development stages.

For acquisition diligence, retain:
- prompts/instructions that materially generated or transformed code;
- exported project archives from AI builders;
- account records showing who controlled the relevant project;
- the terms of service applicable to the relevant tool at the time the output was generated;
- Git history showing subsequent human/project-owner control and modifications.

AI assistance does not by itself establish that every component is exclusively owned by the project owner. Third-party tool terms, third-party code, generated assets, and dependency licenses must be audited separately.

## 4. Third-party materials

The following categories are NOT treated as Ducere-owned IP:

- streaming-provider names, trademarks, logos and service marks;
- movie/TV/anime artwork, posters, backdrops, cast images and other third-party imagery;
- third-party metadata;
- availability data supplied by external services;
- open-source libraries and their associated code;
- Supabase, Vercel and other infrastructure software/services.

Each category requires a separate license/provenance record before commercial use.

## 5. Contributor / contractor ownership

Before an acquisition or commercial transfer, obtain written IP assignments from every person or contractor who materially contributed original code, design, artwork, documentation, branding or other copyrightable material.

The final diligence file should contain:
- contributor name;
- contribution;
- date;
- agreement/assignment;
- confirmation that the contributor has authority to grant the rights;
- any retained rights or restrictions.

If no third-party contributors exist, record a founder-only declaration instead.

## 6. Brand clearance — important open issue

The name "Ducere" is already used by unrelated businesses and is also the subject of existing trademark registrations/applications in multiple jurisdictions. Public searches found, among others, active DUCERE marks associated with education/entertainment services and a separate technology business using the Ducere name.

Therefore:

**Do not represent the Ducere name as legally cleared for worldwide commercial use yet.**

A trademark attorney should perform a jurisdiction-specific clearance search, particularly for India and any country in which Ducere will operate, before filing or commercial launch.

India's official IP portal provides public trademark-search and e-filing services:
https://ipindia.gov.in/

## 8. Acquisition principle

The goal is to be able to answer:

> "Who owns every material piece of Ducere, and what rights does Ducere have to every third-party component it uses?"

with documentary evidence rather than assumptions.
